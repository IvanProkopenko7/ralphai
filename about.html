About RalphAI

RalphAI was created for people who buy, sell, and collect Ralph Lauren pieces and need a fast first opinion before making a decision. Instead of guessing from blurry marketplace photos or relying only on memory, users can upload label images and get an instant AI-based assessment.

The project focuses on practical, real-world use. We train the model on varied label examples, then keep improving it as new formats and edge cases appear. That means better coverage for vintage and modern tags, and more reliable confidence scores when the image quality is good.

RalphAI is designed to save time, reduce obvious mistakes, and support the legit-check community. It is a strong assistant, not a replacement for expert judgment, so uncertain cases should still be reviewed manually.
